How this works

Day tabs

Picks are grouped into tabs by match day — Today, Tomorrow, then each further day in the next week that has at least one pick. Click a tab to see that day's board. Within each day the picks are split into two confidence tiers.

The two confidence tiers

Picks (65–85%) are the bread-and-butter band — the model's pick-side probability clears the 65% floor where the math works out (70% on grass) without being extreme. High-confidence ≥85% — price-shop are the matches where the model is very strong; shop these hardest, because a good price turns a strong read into a strong bet.

The ATP-rank value signal

Each pick shows both players' current ATP ranks. When the model backs the lower-ranked player (a bigger rank number), it flags Model picks the lower-ranked player. Those are the highest-value spots to price-shop: the market and the bookmakers tend to over-price the higher-ranked name, so the odds on the model's pick are often generous.

What "1U / 2U / 3U" means

Stake size based on the model's confidence in its pick. Pick your unit size once and stick with it — most amateur bettors use 1U = 1% of bankroll.

1UModel confidence below 0.68 — the smallest stake
2UModel confidence in the gate band (≥ 0.68)
3UHigh-confidence band (≥ 0.75)

What "look for odds X or better" means

The minimum decimal odds (with the American moneyline equivalent beside it) where the bet still has positive expected value, after a small safety margin. If the best price you can find across books is below this number, skip the bet — nobody's offering enough.

Polymarket & odds are shopping aids, not a gate

We shop each pick across many sportsbooks, not just Polymarket, and Polymarket prices are too volatile to use as an edge check — so they're shown for information only and never decide whether a match is a pick. The small ⓘ next to the price opens a tooltip with the Polymarket volume, when the price was pulled, a link to the Polymarket event, and a Google "betting odds" search link to compare books. Matches with no Polymarket market still get the Google odds-search link.

What's in the "All of today's predictions" table

The full panel of Challenger matches the model scored today — not just the bettable picks. Includes matches that fell below the 65% confidence gate and matches excluded because a player has too little recent history.

How refreshing works

The page is static — it doesn't update on its own, and there's no button here that recomputes anything. New picks are published by hand: I run the full pipeline on my own machine (scrape today's Challenger results, rebuild the Elo ratings, refit the residual model, regenerate the picks JSON, render this HTML) and push the new version to the public site repo. None of that runs in the cloud. To see the latest published picks, just reload the page.

Because publishing is manual, the page can lag reality between runs. If the "last updated" stamp at the top is older than 12 hours, you'll see a yellow banner flagging exactly how stale the picks you're looking at are — treat that as your cue to check back later rather than bet on a stale board.

Where the picks come from

An ATP-Challenger-specific prediction model built since May 2026. Combines a tennis-Elo rating with a residual layer for surface, recent form, rest, qualifiers, and common-opponent strength. On 2024+2025 Challenger holdout, the model picks the right side ~71% of the time on its bettable shortlist — above the 65% floor where the math works out.
