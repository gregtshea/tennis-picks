How this works

What "1U / 2U / 3U" means

Stake size based on the model's edge vs the market. Pick your unit size once and stick with it. Most amateur bettors use 1U = 1% of bankroll.

1UEdge < 5pp or no market price — the smallest stake
2UModel confidence in the gate band (≥ 0.68) or 5-10pp market edge
3UHigh-confidence band (≥ 0.75) or ≥ 10pp market edge

What "Market backs OPP" means

The red badge fires when the model picks one player but the Polymarket close price has the OTHER player as favourite. These are the most contrarian picks — the model is taking a position the market actively disagrees with. They sort to the very top because the upside is the largest when the model is right.

What "look for odds X or better" means

The minimum decimal odds where the bet still has positive expected value, with a small safety margin. If the best price you can find is below this number, skip the bet — the market isn't offering enough.

Why some picks have "no market"

Polymarket doesn't list every Challenger match — only ones with enough trading volume. When there's no Polymarket price, the site falls back to ranking the pick against other picks by raw model confidence. That's labelled "rank proxy" with a percentile.

What's in the "All of today's predictions" table

The full panel of Challenger matches the model scored for the day — not just the bettable shortlist. Includes matches that fell below the 65% confidence gate, matches excluded because a player has too little recent history, and the rare "super-confident skip" cases where the model says ≥ 85% (those get auto-skipped because we don't have enough historical data at that band to trust the calibration yet, but you can still see the prediction).

How refreshing works

The page is static — it doesn't update on its own, and there's no button here that recomputes anything. New picks are published by hand: I run the full pipeline on my own machine (scrape today's Challenger results, rebuild the Elo ratings, refit the residual model, regenerate the picks JSON, render this HTML) and push the new version to the public site repo. None of that runs in the cloud. To see the latest published picks, just reload the page.

Because publishing is manual, the page can lag reality between runs. If the "last updated" stamp at the top is older than 12 hours, you'll see a yellow banner flagging exactly how stale the picks you're looking at are — treat that as your cue to check back later rather than bet on a stale board.

Where the picks come from

An ATP-Challenger-specific prediction model built since May 2026. Combines a tennis-Elo rating with a residual layer for surface, recent form, rest, qualifiers, and common-opponent strength. On 2024+2025 Challenger holdout, the model picks the right side ~71% of the time on its bettable shortlist — above the 65% floor where the math works out.
